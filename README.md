# Finance Portfolio Projects

A portfolio of financial, investment and technical work for employment and graduate applications.

## Current project

[03 Microsoft Three Statement Model](projects/03_microsoft_three_statement/README.md) — in progress.

The agreed historical period is **FY2017–FY2026**, exactly ten fiscal years, with forecasts for FY2027–FY2031. FY2016 is an opening-balance reference only.

## Agreed first sequence

1. Three Statement Model from Primary Filings.
2. DCF and Reverse DCF.
3. Comparable Company Analysis.
4. Equity Research Initiation with a Variant View.

## Delivery standard

Use real primary datasets from leading American or European companies. Russian company data may be used only when expressly approved for a particular case. Each case must include its sources, assumptions, calculations, validation and professional interpretation.

Deliverables are in English; collaboration and explanations to the owner are in Russian. Every project must have a portable archive suitable for Google Drive or Yandex Drive. Before each new project, ask five substantive scoping questions and recommend relevant plugins.

## Scope decisions

- Projects 02 and 25 are separate DCF cases on two different companies in adjacent industries.
- Project 01 requires the owner's own investment view, elicited through an interview. Project 33 is an updated study after new reporting or a study of another company.
- Project 37 compares strategies using the Sharpe ratio and explains its limitations.
- Projects 42–45 become distinct professional cases with real data, calculations and a conclusion. They must not duplicate the other portfolio projects.
- Web applications may be developed as later stages where appropriate.
